What each status means

Approval and payment verification are manual — announced in the WhatsApp group. Your record updates here when its state changes.

REGISTRATION

  • ● SUBMITTED — received, awaiting review
  • ◐ PENDING REVIEW — being checked by organizers
  • ✔ APPROVED — slot confirmed (organizers only)
  • ✕ REJECTED — not accepted, reason via WhatsApp
  • ○ CANCELLED — withdrawn

PAYMENT

  • ○ PAYMENT PENDING — no reference yet
  • ◐ PAYMENT SUBMITTED — reference received, not verified
  • ✔ PAYMENT VERIFIED — confirmed by organizers only
  • ✕ PAYMENT REJECTED — problem with the payment
